# Publication boundary

This package is intentionally clean-room and public-safe in structure.

Included:

- general intake-integrity principles;
- synthetic fixtures;
- generic sales roles and fields;
- newly written reference code;
- generic 1C integration contracts based on public platform capabilities;
- executable product invariants.

Excluded:

- employer/client source files and code;
- internal task numbers, product names and credentials;
- original legal copy;
- real customer records;
- proprietary matching thresholds/algorithms;
- internal metrics, commercial forecasts and confidential process details.

The 1C examples contain placeholder entity/field names. They are not extracts from a real configuration.
